About Me and Hex and Shine

My name is Vanessa, I grew up in San Diego CA and have lived here all of my life, Ive ran a collection of businesses and have served as a property manager for quite some time. During all of my various jobs one thing has always stuck with me: I have a thing for clean, structure organization and cleanliness just tickles me and I wanted to be able to share my cleaning and organization with that touch of sparkling and smelly good for everyone to enjoy.

In case you couldn't tell I also have a flare for the alternative, my favorite holiday is halloween and my favorite style is eclectic, moody maximalist. I love collectors and currators and tend to the things people hold dear with the utmost of care and precision. I believe in providing the best service I can, emphasizing quality and compassionate care of a home or business as if it were mine and I was having the Queen of England for an extended stay.

This extends to far more than just my cleaning, I believe in quality care with any job I undertake, and those who work with me extend the same values. It was with this mind set and the passion for the things I love that I created Hex and Shine. A full service cleaning business with a penchant for quality and a flash of witchiness.
